## 🇨🇳 中文

为商家、客服和 Bot 配置人员生成电商在线文字客服的可执行处理方案与正式交付文档。输入一个客户问题——售前咨询、催发货、物流履约、退换货、质量问题、补偿争议、差评投诉、私聊舆情询问，甚至一张聊天截图——输出能直接执行的结果：问题判断、首选策略、可发送话术、内部操作步骤、条件分支和权限边界。按需形成文档、SOP 或自动客服配置，并始终区分「使用者（商家或客服团队）」和「客户（咨询、催单或投诉的一方）」。电话客服、线下接待、媒体公关、客服培训质检、真实系统操作与法律责任最终认定不在适用范围内。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入客户问题 → 输出可直接发送的话术 + 内部操作清单
- 覆盖催发货、物流、退换货、质量问题、补偿争议与差评投诉
- 聊天截图分析：从对话还原诉求、时间线与责任边界
- 条件分支与升级规则：什么情况转主管、谁能批补偿
- 权限矩阵：写清客服可做与不可做的操作
- 交付 SOP、正式文档或自动客服 Bot 配置

## 🇬🇧 English

Generates executable handling plans and formal delivery documents for ecommerce online text customer service. Give it one customer situation — pre-sale questions, shipping chasers, logistics fulfillment, returns and exchanges, quality complaints, compensation disputes, negative reviews, private-chat reputation inquiries, or a chat screenshot — and it returns something you can act on: the problem read, the preferred strategy, ready-to-send replies, internal action steps, conditional branches and permission limits. Output can be packaged as a document, an SOP or an auto-service bot configuration, and it always separates the requester (merchant or CS team) from the customer (the one asking, chasing or complaining). Phone support, offline reception, media PR, training audits, live system operations and final legal rulings are out of scope.

### ✨ What it does

- One customer problem in; send-ready replies plus an internal action list out
- Covers shipping chases, logistics, returns, quality issues, compensation and bad reviews
- Chat-screenshot analysis recovers the real request, timeline and ownership
- Conditional branches and escalation rules for supervisor handoff and approvals
- A permission matrix stating what support may and may not do
- Delivers SOPs, formal documents or auto-service bot configuration
